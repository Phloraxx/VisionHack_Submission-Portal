// PRIORITY ISSUES
P0

EventMark draws brackets, not VH

app/components/shared/event-mark.tsx, lines 60–84. The SVG paths are M6 10 L20 22 L6 34 (a `<` chevron, brand fill) and M20 22 L34 10 M20 22 L34 34 (a `>` chevron, chrome stroke). The component docstring says "geometric monogram for VH built from two interlocking chevrons" — but two opposing chevrons are not a monogram for VH. They read as brackets, an aperture, or a vision frame.

FIX: /design refine — either (a) update SVG to draw a true VH monogram, or (b) update the docstring + rename to acknowledge the chevron is an intentional aperture mark. Pick one.

app/routes/lead/dashboard.tsx, lines 271–281. The hero strip says "Your team X is on step N of 3". The metric card row immediately below has a card labeled "Steps completed" with value "N / 3". Both surfaces report the same information.

FIX: /design refine — cut "step N of 3" from the hero mission line. Replace with a concrete next action ("Complete the questionnaire to unlock submission").
